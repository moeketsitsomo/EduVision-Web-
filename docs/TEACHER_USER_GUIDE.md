# EduVision School Website — Teacher User Guide

This guide is for teachers who use the EduVision School CMS.

---

## Logging in

1. Open the school website.
2. Go to `/admin/login`.
3. Enter your school email and password.
4. Your account must have role `TEACHER` or `SCHOOL_STAFF`.

---

## Teacher dashboard

The dashboard shows quick links and counts for:
- Students in your classes.
- Attendance registers.
- Homework and assessments.
- Notices and messages.

---

## Students

- View student lists under **CMS > Students**.
- Search by name, grade or student number.
- View contact details and parent links.

---

## Attendance

1. Go to **CMS > Attendance**.
2. Select the grade/class and date.
3. Mark each student as Present, Absent, Late or Excused.
4. Save. Parents can see the updated attendance in the portal.

---

## Homework

1. Go to **CMS > Homework**.
2. Create a new homework task.
3. Set the title, description, due date and linked subject.
4. Optionally attach a file URL from the Media Library.
5. Publish. Learners and parents see it in the portal.

---

## Results

1. Go to **CMS > Results**.
2. Select the assessment, subject and grade.
3. Enter marks for each learner.
4. Save. Reports can be generated from the data.

---

## Subjects

- View subjects under **CMS > Subjects**.
- Make sure each subject has a name, code and grade range.
- Mark subjects `isPublished` to show them on the public Academics page.

---

## Notices

1. Go to **CMS > Notices**.
2. Write a title and message.
3. Choose an audience: All, Parents, Teachers, Learners or Staff.
4. Set `isPublished` and `publishedAt`.
5. Save. The notice appears on the public site and portal.

---

## Timetable

- View the school timetable under **CMS > Timetable**.
- Check your class schedule and venue.
- Contact the timetable administrator to request changes.

---

## Communication

- Use **CMS > Communication** for internal messages.
- Send announcements to staff or parents.
- Keep messages professional and within school policy.

---

## Media Library

- Upload teaching resources and photos.
- Use categories to keep files organised.
- Copy URLs to share documents with students.

---

## Best practices

- Log out after each session, especially on shared computers.
- Do not share passwords.
- Contact the school admin if you cannot access a module.
