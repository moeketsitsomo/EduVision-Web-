# EduVision School Website — Parent / Learner Portal Guide

This guide is for parents and learners who use the public Parent/Learner Portal.

---

## Accessing the portal

1. Open the school's public website.
2. Click the **Portal** button in the top navigation.
3. Enter your email and password.
4. If you do not have login details, contact the school office.

Example portal URL: `https://yourschool.edu/portal`

---

## Portal dashboard

After login you see a summary of:
- New notices.
- Attendance rate.
- Average results.
- Homework due.

Use the tabs on the left or top to open each section.

---

## Notices

- Read school announcements.
- Notices may be for all parents, specific grades or learners.
- Unread notices are highlighted on the dashboard.

---

## Calendar

- View the school calendar.
- See events, sports days, parent meetings and holidays.
- Click an event for time and location details.

---

## Attendance

- View attendance records for the term.
- See total days present, absent and late.
- Contact the school if an absence is marked incorrectly.

---

## Reports

- Download or view term reports.
- Reports may include marks, comments and class averages.
- Some reports are password-protected PDFs.

---

## Homework

- See homework assigned to the learner.
- Check due dates and submission status.
- Download attachments if provided.

---

## Profile and password

- Update your profile under **Settings** if available.
- Change your password regularly.
- Do not share your login details.

---

## Getting help

If you cannot log in or see incorrect information:
- Click **Contact** on the public website.
- Fill in the contact form.
- The school office will respond via email or phone.
